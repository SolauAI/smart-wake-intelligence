# Heart Rate and HRV

## HRV
A 2025 meta-analysis found autonomic changes following sleep deprivation, including reductions in RMSSD.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40895095/

Experimental wearable research also demonstrates autonomic changes during sleep restriction.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33897355/

## Interpretation
HRV can provide supporting evidence about physiological state, especially as a deviation from the user's own baseline.

It is not a direct measure of:
- total sleep need;
- sleep debt in hours;
- readiness to wake at a specific minute.

## Heart rate
Overnight and resting heart rate can provide additional context, but is confounded by many factors.

## Product rule
Use HR/HRV as one part of a multi-signal model. Never use a universal HRV threshold to decide alarm time.
