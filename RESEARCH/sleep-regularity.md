# Sleep Regularity

Systematic reviews find associations between sleep regularity and health outcomes, though observational evidence does not prove simple causality.

Sources:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/41259946/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33054339/

## Product consequence
Do not optimize each night independently.

A wake decision that saves 45 minutes tonight but systematically creates irregular sleep may be inferior to a slightly later, more regular schedule.

## Features
Track:
- sleep onset variability;
- wake-time variability;
- sleep-duration variability;
- schedule deviation from personal norm.

Regularity should be a longitudinal constraint/supporting objective, not an opaque penalty that overrides safety.
