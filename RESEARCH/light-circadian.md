# Light and Circadian Support

A meta-analysis of 29 studies / 1,210 participants found that light characteristics and timing can influence alertness.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/35142669/

## Product opportunity
The user may sleep with shutters closed, removing natural morning light as a wake cue.

A future version could coordinate:
- phone screen/light;
- smart bulbs;
- dedicated sunrise lighting.

## Important distinction
Light should be treated as a circadian/alertness intervention, not evidence that a person has completed their required sleep.

The system should not use artificial light to justify unsafe sleep reduction.
