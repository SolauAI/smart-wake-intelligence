# Smart Alarm Evidence

A 2020 study evaluated simplified sleep-stage prediction for smart alarms and reported moderate classification performance in its protocol.

https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33018943/

## Interpretation
This supports the feasibility of attempting stage-aware wake selection, but it does not establish that a Garmin-based consumer product can reliably identify the best wake minute.

## Product consequence
The strongest product validation must be outcome-based:
- Did the person wake more easily?
- Was next-day performance preserved?
- Was sleep need protected?
- Did regularity improve or remain stable?

The alarm should not be marketed as "waking you at the perfect sleep-cycle moment."
