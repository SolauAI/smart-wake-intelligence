# Sleep Stages and Wake Inertia

## Sleep cycles
Cycle duration varies between individuals and across nights. The 90-minute rule is not suitable as a deterministic alarm algorithm.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/37914631/

## Sleep inertia
Experimental evidence indicates that waking from deeper sleep can be associated with greater sleep inertia.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/12531174/

## Wearable limitation
Consumer wearables estimate stages indirectly. Their agreement with PSG is limited and device-specific.

Sources:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/39484805/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/40303381/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/38557808/

## Product rule
Stage data may help choose between otherwise similar candidate wake moments, but should not override sleep sufficiency, recovery or safety constraints.
