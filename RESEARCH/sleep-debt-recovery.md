# Sleep Restriction and Recovery

Repeated sleep restriction can create cumulative neurobehavioral deficits and recovery may take more than one recovery night.

Sources:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/33274389/
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/23941878/

## Product consequence
The model must use multi-night history.

A single night that looks "good" should not erase evidence of repeated prior restriction.

## Safety metric
Maintain rolling measures such as:
- recent sleep duration versus personal baseline;
- number of short nights;
- cumulative restriction proxy;
- recovery-night history.

These are model features, not clinical diagnoses.
