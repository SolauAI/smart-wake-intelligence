# Sleep Duration Evidence

## What is established
Adults have individual sleep needs. AASM/SRS recommends at least 7 hours regularly for adults 18–60, while acknowledging individual variation and contexts where more than 9 hours may be appropriate.

Source:
https://www.aasm.org/resources/pdf/adultsleepdurationconsensus.pdf

## Product interpretation
The app must not treat 7 hours as an optimization target. The relevant question is whether a particular wake time is compatible with the individual's established need and current recovery state.

## Individual differences
Sleep restriction studies show large and stable differences in vulnerability between people.

Source:
https://pubmed.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/17118094/

## Key design consequence
The model needs a personalized sleep-duration distribution rather than one universal threshold.

## Validation requirement
Any claim that the app can safely reduce a user's usual sleep opportunity must be tested against next-day performance and longitudinal outcomes.
