# Product Vision

A fixed alarm far enough after bedtime can protect against insufficient sleep but may preserve more time in bed than an individual needs on every night.

The product asks:

> Given recent sleep and tonight's signals, what is the earliest time I can wake without unnecessarily sacrificing recovery or long-term sleep need?

The user defines earliest and latest acceptable wake times. The system handles the inference.

This is not "wake at the end of a 90-minute cycle" and not "wake after exactly 7 hours".

The promise is:

**Wake as early as is reasonably safe and useful, within your limits, using evidence from your own sleep history and tonight's signals.**

The UI should show selected wake time, confidence, a short explanation and fallback status. Avoid fake-precision medical scores.
