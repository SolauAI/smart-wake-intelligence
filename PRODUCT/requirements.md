# Product Requirements

## P0
- User-defined earliest and latest wake times.
- Reliable fallback alarm.
- Garmin ingestion for supported configuration.
- Longitudinal sleep history.
- Personal baseline calculation.
- Confidence-aware wake decision.
- Decision logging.
- Morning outcome feedback.
- Manual override.
- Clear indication when adaptive mode could not operate.

## P1
- Reaction-time test.
- Daytime energy/sleepiness feedback.
- Context tags for illness, alcohol, unusual training, travel and stress.
- Historical explanation of decisions.
- Regularity tracking.
- Model calibration dashboard.

## P2
- Morning-light integration.
- Connected smart lights.
- Multi-device wearable support.
- More sophisticated personalized models.
- Experimental wake-inertia prediction.

## Non-functional
- Alarm reliability has priority over optimization.
- Robustness to connectivity failures.
- Privacy by design and data minimization.
- Full audit trail for model decisions.
- Explainability for user-visible decisions.
